1. Executive Summary
This project is a lightweight, domain-specific CRM for a small-scale feed business. It is designed to work alongside Tally rather than replace it. Tally remains the accounting and financial source of truth; the CRM becomes the operational system for customer relationships, daily follow-up, WhatsApp communication, customer feedback, feed-grade requirements and simple action-oriented intelligence.
The system is intentionally designed to remain small, fast and easy to use. Advanced AI scoring, complex customer-health models, marketing automation and enterprise CRM features are deliberately postponed until sufficient reliable CRM data has accumulated.
2. Product Principles
Tally = accounting and financial source of truth.
CRM = customer relationship, interaction, follow-up and market-demand intelligence.
WhatsApp = primary communication channel for fast customer contact, initially through controlled deep links.
Requirements = structured record of what customers actually need.
Today's Work = primary operating screen that answers what needs attention today.
Supabase/PostgreSQL = central application database; SQLite is not part of the production architecture.
Raw Tally data must be preserved before normalization or identity resolution.
Ambiguous customer identity must be reviewed by a human; uncertain parties must never be silently merged.
Every sprint is independently testable and requires explicit Product Owner approval before the next sprint begins.
3. Architecture & Technology Baseline
3.1 Technology
Frontend: Flutter with a responsive architecture suitable for desktop/web and future mobile clients.
Backend/Data Platform: Supabase.
Database: PostgreSQL through Supabase.
Authentication: Supabase Auth or an equivalent approved authentication layer, introduced only when required by the current sprint.
Tally integration: controlled import pipeline with staging and normalization layers.
WhatsApp: human-controlled WhatsApp deep links initially; official API automation is explicitly out of the initial scope.

5.1 Important Identity Rules
CRM Party Identity is the stable business identity used by the CRM.
Tally Ledger Name is a source-system display value and may change.
A CRM party can be linked to one or more Tally ledger identities only after identity resolution.
A possible duplicate is not a confirmed duplicate.
Merging must be an explicit, auditable action.
Original Tally values must remain recoverable from staging/import history.
5.2 Status Model
Tally Status is separate from CRM Status.
Tally Status:
  Active
  OLD
  Unknown
CRM Status:
  Active
  Dormant
  At Risk
  Inactive
  Blocked
Tally OLD must not automatically mean CRM Dormant. The two statuses describe different systems.
5.3 Communication Preferences
Communication Preference:
  WhatsApp
  Call
  WhatsApp + Call
  No WhatsApp
  Do Not Contact
Preferred Contact Time: optional
6. Tally Transactional Data Rule
Critical rule: 
The supplied ledger-list PDF is not sufficient for historical financial metrics such as last purchase, purchase frequency, sales trend, purchase value or outstanding balance.
Those metrics require an appropriate voucher-level or transaction-level Tally export. Antigravity must not infer transaction history from a ledger-list PDF.
Ledger List / Party Master
        → identity + master-data use
Voucher-Level Export
        → transaction / financial intelligence
7. Tally Import & Identity Resolution Rules
Preserve the original Tally ledger name exactly as imported.
Do not use party name alone as a permanent primary identity.
Detect spelling variations, punctuation differences, abbreviations, location suffixes and aliases.
Flag ledgers containing '(OLD)' for appropriate Tally status handling.
Support Exact Match, Possible Match, Manual Confirmation and New Party outcomes.
Never silently merge uncertain parties.
Keep import history so a problematic import can be investigated or reprocessed.
Use staging tables before CRM tables.

SPRINT 2 — Tally Import, Staging & Party Identity Resolution
Objective: 
Safely bring Tally party data into the CRM without corrupting or silently merging identities.
Build

Implement import pipeline appropriate to the verified source format.
Preserve original Tally ledger name, source reference, group and Tally status.
Normalize data without destroying raw values.
Detect spelling variations, aliases, location variants and '(OLD)' records.
Create party identity links.
Create manual identity review/deduplication queue.
Support Exact Match, Possible Match, Manual Confirmation and New Party.

Scope boundary: 
Do not calculate transaction history from the ledger-list PDF. Do not auto-merge uncertain parties.
GATE 2 — 
STOP. User reviews imported records, duplicate candidates, OLD records and match decisions.
SPRINT 3 — Today's Work & Daily Follow-up
Objective: 
Make the CRM useful for daily customer action.
Build

Show today's follow-ups.
Show overdue follow-ups.
Show high-priority actions.

Do not add advanced scoring or complex analytics.
GATE 3 — 
STOP. User can open Today's Work and immediately know whom to contact and what action is due.
SPRINT 4 — One-Tap WhatsApp + Interaction Feedback
Objective: 
Make customer communication fast while keeping human control.
Build
Create WhatsApp deep-link action.
Use editable pre-filled message templates.
After the user sends/returns from WhatsApp, immediately present quick feedback options.
Feedback options: Requirement, Order, Price Issue, Quality Issue, Payment Issue, No Requirement, Call Later, Competitor, Other.

If Requirement is selected, open requirement capture automatically.
If Call Later is selected, prompt for follow-up date automatically.
Respect Do Not Contact and communication preferences.
Scope boundary: 
Do not build uncontrolled bulk messaging, marketing automation or official WhatsApp API integration.
GATE 4 — 
STOP. User can contact a customer and record the outcome in seconds.

Objective: 
Capture real customer demand in structured form.
Build
Create requirement from interaction or customer profile.
Capture customer, product, quantity, unit, expected rate, required date, priority and notes.
Link requirement to source interaction.
Implement statuses: New, Quotation Required, Quotation Sent, Negotiation, Confirmed, Lost, Closed.
Record status history.
Allow next follow-up from the requirement.
Search/filter open requirements.
Keep product master simple and domain-specific.
Scope boundary: 
Do not build complex quotation, order-management or ERP functionality unless separately approved.
GATE 5 — 
STOP. Complete Customer → Contact → Requirement → Follow-up workflow using a real example.

Objective: 
Improve actionability without turning the product into a large BI platform.
Build
Show open requirements.
Show pending quotations.
Show overdue follow-ups.
Show customers contacted today.
Show dormant/at-risk candidates only from transparent business rules.
Show simple requirement demand by product/category.
Show expected business only where the underlying data is reliable.

Scope boundary: 
No AI scoring, advanced customer health models or predictive analytics.
GATE 6 — 
STOP. User confirms the screen helps decide what to do today.

Objective: 
Introduce historical financial intelligence only after the correct Tally transaction export is available.

Support appropriate transaction-level metrics such as last purchase, purchase frequency, purchase value or outstanding only when source data supports them.
Keep financial/Tally information clearly separate from CRM interaction data.

Do not fabricate metrics from the ledger-list PDF. Do not make CRM the accounting source of truth.

12. Strict Antigravity Control Protocol
The user is the Product Owner. Antigravity is the development agent.
Work ONLY on the current sprint.
Never implement future-sprint functionality because it appears convenient or related.
Never change the approved architecture, database strategy or major dependencies without explicit approval.
Do not introduce SQLite as a production database.
Do not fabricate, infer or invent missing Tally information.
Never silently merge ambiguous parties.
Never delete or overwrite raw Tally staging information.
All destructive or identity-changing operations must be explicit and auditable.
Every sprint must produce a working, testable increment.
Every sprint must include regression testing of previously working functionality.
A sprint is not complete merely because code compiles.
At sprint completion, generate the Sprint Completion Report.
At sprint completion, STOP.
Do not automatically start the next sprint.
Only explicit Product Owner approval permits the next sprint.
